# Major and Minor Degree

## 13.1 Minor Discipline

Students may opt for a Minor in a discipline other than the discipline in which they were originally admitted.

The Minor will be mentioned in:

- The degree certificate
- The final grade card

The original branch remains the student's Major.

For example:

**Major:** Electronics and Communication Engineering  
**Minor:** Computer Science and Engineering

## 13.2 Requirements for Minor

A four-year B.Tech degree with a Minor in a specific domain requires:

- Completion of the four-year degree programme.
- Completion of **172 credits**.
- Satisfaction of the prescribed credit requirements.
- Earning **8 additional credits** through 2 Open Elective Courses (OECs).
- Earning a minimum of **5 OECs**, including the 2 additional OECs, from the basket of courses prescribed for the particular Minor.

## 13.3 Minor Course Structure

For every Minor discipline, the courses are finalized by the Senate.

The courses are proposed by the IUPC based on the recommendation of the DUPC of the concerned department.

## 13.4 Eligibility to Apply

Only students who have completed all credits required in their first two semesters are eligible to apply for a Minor discipline.

## 13.5 Application

Students must apply for a Minor using the prescribed form.

The Academic Section calls for applications at the end of the second semester of each academic year.

Completed applications must be submitted by the last date specified in the notification.

## 13.6 Intake

The intake for every Minor discipline is decided by the Academic Section in consultation with the respective Head of Department.

## 13.7 Choice of Minor

Students may list their preferred Minor disciplines in order of preference.

Once the application has been submitted, the choices cannot be changed.

## 13.8 Selection

Selection to Minor disciplines is based strictly on merit according to the CGPA of applicants, depending on:

- Available intake
- Number of applications received

## 13.9 Starting the Minor

The Minor becomes effective from the student's **third semester**.

No change of Minor discipline is permitted after this.

## 13.10 Dropping an Open Elective

A student will not be eligible for the Minor degree if the student drops an Open Elective and subsequently takes another Open Elective from a different basket.

## 13.11 Minor Backlog

A backlog course in the Minor discipline may be cleared during the summer term through a supplementary examination.

Failing again in the supplementary examination will result in dropping of the Minor discipline.

## 13.12 SGPA and CGPA Calculation

For a student pursuing a Minor, after completing at least two semesters:

- Five OECs are associated with the Minor basket.
- The best **3 out of 5 OECs** are considered for CGPA calculation.
- The remaining 2 OECs are not counted in CGPA.
- The CGPA calculation is therefore based on **164 credits** after completing 172 credits.

If necessary, the SGPA of a particular semester may be upgraded when an OEC from that semester is not counted in the CGPA.

The final mark sheet will contain a separate section listing:

- All Minor courses
- The 3 OECs counted in CGPA
- The 2 OECs not counted in CGPA

## 13.13 Dropping the Minor

If the Minor discipline is dropped, successfully completed extra courses may be mentioned in a separate certificate upon the student's request.

## 13.14 Opting Out

A student may opt out of the Minor discipline by submitting the prescribed form.

Once a student opts out, the student cannot rejoin the Minor discipline.
