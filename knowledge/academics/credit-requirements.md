# Credit Requirement and Course Rules

## 12.1 Credit Requirement

To qualify for a B.Tech degree, a student must complete the credit requirement prescribed in the Course Structure for the particular programme.

The credit requirement for a programme is:

**164–172 credits**

### Equivalent Courses

If a student has to take courses different from those prescribed in the Course Structure because of backlog cases, semester-drop cases or similar circumstances, the student must complete the exact number of equivalent courses decided by the DUPC.

### NPTEL/SWAYAM for Theoretical Backlogs

If a student has a backlog in a theoretical course with no laboratory component, the student may opt for a course offered by NPTEL/SWAYAM having equivalent course credit.

The student's progress will be monitored by a nominated faculty member of the concerned department.

Permission in such cases is granted by the IUPC on the recommendation of the DUPC.

## 12.2 Extra Courses and Backlogs

Students may be permitted to take extra courses on the recommendation of the Faculty Advisor and approval of the Department Undergraduate Programme Committee (DUPC).

### 12.2.1 Backlog Courses

Students who have failed with an "F" grade in one or more subjects in previous Autumn/Spring semesters may register in the next semester for:

**At most two theory backlog courses**

This is allowed only if the courses are offered in that semester.

A student with due backlog courses may register for Summer School for:

**At most 10 credits**

subject to the applicable provisions and course availability.

### 12.2.2 Semester Drop

A student who has dropped a semester because of critical disease, accident or calamity will be allowed to register for the subsequent semester.

Such a student may be allowed to register for extra courses, including backlog courses, subject to the limits specified in the ordinance:

- Maximum two theory courses; or
- Two theory courses with laboratory components.

This applies when the corresponding courses are offered.

### 12.2.3 Dropping PEC/OEC

Students are allowed to drop only:

- One Program Elective Course (PEC); or
- One Open Elective Course (OEC)

per semester.

An equivalent or alternate course may be permitted in a later semester.

## 12.3 Curriculum and Syllabi

Every undergraduate programme has a curriculum and syllabi for courses approved by the Senate.

The DUPC discusses and recommends undergraduate course syllabi before sending them to the Institute Undergraduate Programme Committee (IUPC).

The IUPC considers departmental proposals and recommends them to the Senate for consideration and approval.

## 12.4 Medium of Instruction

The medium of:

- Instruction
- Examination
- Project reports

shall be **English**.
