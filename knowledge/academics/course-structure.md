# B.Tech Course Structure

## 11.1 Course Structure

A student shall register for theory and practical courses each semester according to the respective Course Structure prescribed by the Senate.

Each course carries a weight in terms of credit units depending on the nature of the course, such as theory or practical.

The credit is determined by the expected contact hours, including:

- Lecture hours
- Tutorial hours
- Laboratory hours
- Additional hours that a student is expected to devote per week

## L-T-P-C Format

Every course is associated with a four-tuple:

**L-T-P-C**

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L | Number of Lecture Hours per week |
| T | Number of Tutorial Hours per week |
| P | Number of Practical/Laboratory Hours per week |
| C | Credit of the course |

## 11.2 General Credit Pattern

Except for Internship/Projects, credits are assigned according to the following general pattern:

| Activity | Credits |
|---|---:|
| Each Lecture period per week | 1 credit |
| Each Tutorial period per week | 1 credit |
| Three-hour Practical/Laboratory Session per week | 3 credits |
| Two-hour Practical/Laboratory Session per week | 2 credits |
