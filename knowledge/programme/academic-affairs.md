# Academic Affairs Administration

The Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs is responsible for implementing academic decisions taken by the Institute Undergraduate Program Committee (IUPC).

The office of the Dean (Academic) is responsible for:

- receiving, processing and maintaining records related to undergraduate programmes;
- maintaining information on curricula and courses offered;
- maintaining records concerning the academic calendar, admission and registration;
- maintaining semester leave and examination records;
- handling records concerning unfair-means matters, grades, and awards of degrees, medals and prizes;
- disseminating information about academic matters;
- issuing necessary memoranda and orders;
- acting as a communication channel between students, instructors, departments, interdisciplinary programmes, schools, cells, centres and the IUPC.

The Undergraduate Section of the Dean (Academic)'s office assists the IUPC in its functioning.
