# Late Registration

A student who does not register on the day or days announced in the Academic Calendar may be permitted to complete late registration when there is a compelling reason.

Late registration:
- must occur within the period notified by the Institute;
- requires payment of the prescribed late fee;
- normally cannot be permitted after one week, or after the deadline specified in the Academic Calendar.

The applicable deadline is therefore the one notified in the Institute's Academic Calendar.
