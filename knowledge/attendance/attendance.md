# Attendance Requirements

## 8.1 General Attendance

Students are required to attend all classes for which they have registered.

This includes:

- Lectures
- Tutorials
- Laboratories
- Practical sessions
- Workshops

## 8.2 Minimum Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of **75%**, taking medical, calamity and disaster-related absence into consideration.

The following consequences apply:

| Attendance | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Below 50% | The student is debarred from appearing in the End-Semester Examination and is awarded an "F" grade in the course. |
| 50% to below 75% | One grade is reduced from the student's secured grade in the concerned course, except when the secured grade is "P". |
| 75% or above | The minimum attendance requirement is satisfied, subject to the other provisions of the ordinance. |

## 8.3 Temporary Absence for Approved Activities

A student's temporary absence for activities such as:

- Technical festivals
- Technical competitions
- Cultural meets
- Sports meets
- Academic workshops
- Academic conferences
- Research visits

may be condoned for attendance.

Such absence is subject to recommendation by the Faculty in-charge of Student Affairs or Faculty in-charge of Academic Affairs and the Head of the concerned department, followed by approval by the Director.
