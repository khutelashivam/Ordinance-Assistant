# Multiple Exit and Re-Entry

Section 12.5 provides multiple exit options from the undergraduate programme.

## Exit-1: After First Year

A student who exits after completing the first year and has secured a minimum of:

**40 credits**

may receive a **UG Certificate** if the student also earns:

**4 credits**

through one of the following during the first-year summer vacation:

- Two-month summer internship
- ITI-level courses
- Skill-based courses

## Exit-2: After Second Year

A student who exits after completing the second year and has secured a minimum of:

**80 credits**

may receive a **UG Diploma** if the student also earns:

**4 credits**

through one of the following during the second-year summer vacation:

- Two-month summer internship
- Diploma-level courses
- Skill-based courses

## Exit-3: After Third Year

A student who exits after completing the third year and has secured a minimum of:

**120 credits**

may receive a:

**B.Voc Degree in the Major discipline**

The student must additionally complete a:

**4-credit summer internship**

during the third-year summer vacation.

## Exit-4: Four-Year B.Tech

### B.Tech

The four-year B.Tech degree in the Major discipline is awarded to students who:

- Complete the four-year degree programme.
- Complete 164 credits.
- Satisfy the credit requirements.

### B.Tech (Honours)

The four-year B.Tech (Honours) degree in the Major discipline is awarded to students who:

- Complete the four-year degree programme.
- Complete 172 credits.
- Have a CGPA of 8.0 or more, excluding Additional Credits.

Detailed Honours regulations are given in Section 14.

### B.Tech with Major and Minor

The four-year B.Tech degree with a minor in a specific domain is awarded to students who:

- Complete the four-year degree programme.
- Complete 172 credits.
- Satisfy the applicable credit requirements.

Detailed Minor regulations are given in Section 13.

## Re-Entry

Students using the multiple-exit provisions are allowed to re-enter the degree programme within:

**3 years**

They must complete the degree programme within the stipulated maximum period of:

**7 years**
