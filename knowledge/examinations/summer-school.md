# Summer School

## 16.1 Eligibility

Students who are eligible to appear for a supplementary examination in a particular course are also eligible to register for that course in the Summer School.

The Summer School normally runs from:

**Last week of May or first week of June to the last week of July.**

## Minimum Number of Students

A Summer School for a particular course can be conducted only when:

- At least **5 eligible candidates** have applied for that course.
- Suitable and available faculty members are found to conduct the course.

## Assessment

Assessment of a Summer School course is similar to the assessment followed in:

- Autumn Semester
- Spring Semester

The concerned department retains the semester performance records of the students.

## 16.2 Offering a Summer School Course

A department may offer a Summer School course:

1. On the recommendation of the DUPC.
2. With approval of the Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs.

A student may register for a maximum of:

**12 credits**

during a summer term.

## 16.3 Announcement and Registration

Summer School courses are announced by the Academic Affairs Office at the end of the even semester before the commencement of the End-Semester Examination.

Students must register for Summer School courses by:

- Paying the prescribed fees.
- Registering within the stipulated time specified in the announcement.

## 16.4 Semester Drop Students

Students who have been granted a semester drop by the Chairman, Senate, on grounds such as:

- Medical reasons
- Calamity
- Natural disaster

may be allowed to clear the concerned courses during the Summer Term.

The applicable conditions are those specified under Clause 12.2.2.
