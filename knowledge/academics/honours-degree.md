# B.Tech Honours Degree

## 14.1 Requirements

A student who wishes to pursue the B.Tech (Honours) degree must:

- Earn **8 additional credits**.
- Have a **CGPA of 8.0 or more**, excluding Additional Credits.
- Complete the prescribed undergraduate research work.

## 14.2 Undergraduate Research and Additional Courses

### Research Requirement

At least **4 of the 8 additional credits** must be earned through Undergraduate Research Work.

The research work must result in the publication of at least **one research paper** in an SCI/SCIE/SCOPUS journal in a relevant area.

The assigned guide/supervisor(s) from the Institute will be co-author(s).

The total number of students who are co-authors of the paper cannot be more than **two**.

There cannot be any other co-author from inside or outside the Institute.

### Remaining 4 Credits

The remaining **4 additional credits** may be completed through Program Elective Courses (PEC).

These courses may be completed through:

- SWAYAM
- NPTEL

The additional course must not be equivalent to:

- A course already taken by the student; or
- A course that the student will later take as a requirement for completing the B.Tech degree.

## 14.3 CGPA Calculation

The additional **8 credits** earned for the Honours degree are **not considered** while calculating CGPA.
