# B.Tech Course Categories

The B.Tech curriculum contains the following categories of courses.

## AEC — Ability Enhancement Courses

Ability Enhancement Courses are based on content intended to enhance knowledge through various areas of study.

They include:

- Language and Literature
- Indian Knowledge System
- Environmental Science and Sustainable Development

These are mandatory for all disciplines, and some may be chosen from a pool of courses.

**Indicative share: 3%-4%**

## VAC — Value Added Courses

Value Added Courses are intended to inculcate:

- Ethics
- Culture
- Constitutional values
- Soft skills
- Sports education
- Similar areas supporting all-round development

Some courses may be chosen from a pool.

**Indicative share: 3%-4%**

## SEC — Skill Enhancement Courses

Skill Enhancement Courses are skill-based courses intended to provide:

- Hands-on training
- Competencies
- Practical skills

**Indicative share: 2%-3%**

## IEC — Interdisciplinary Engineering Courses

These are basic interdisciplinary engineering courses from areas such as:

- Information Technology
- Electronics
- Automation

The courses are from areas other than the student's programme of study.

**Indicative share: 14%-20%**

For Mathematics and Computing (MNC):

**Indicative share: 25%-30%**

## PCC — Program Core Courses

Program Core Courses are mandatory courses considered essential for the chosen engineering/science discipline.

**Indicative share: 27%-35%**

## PEC — Program Elective Courses

Each branch offers a variety of elective courses.

These courses provide students with opportunities to:

- Discover academic interests
- Choose courses from their programme's elective pool
- Enhance engagement in the learning process

Such courses are termed Program Elective Courses (PEC).

**Indicative share: 9%-14%**

## OEC — Open Elective Courses

Open Elective Courses provide multidisciplinary or interdisciplinary education.

When a student chooses a PEC/PCC offered by another department, that course is treated as an Open Elective Course (OEC) for that student.

**Indicative share: 9%-14%**

## MMC — Mandatory Mathematics Courses

Mandatory Mathematics Courses are required for each discipline except Mathematics and Computing.

**Indicative share: 7%-10%**

## PIR — Project, Internship and Research

PIR includes:

- Engineering projects
- Research projects
- Internships
- Summer internships
- Summer projects

**Indicative share: 9%-14%**
