# Online and External Course Credits

Course credits may be earned in online/offline mode from online portals or other institutes/universities.

Prior approval of the Department Undergraduate Program Committee (DUPC) of the respective department is required.

The ordinance specifies the following category-wise maximum limits:

| Course Category | Maximum Credits | Permitted Sources |
|---|---:|---|
| VAC/AEC | 11 credits | SWAYAM, NPTEL, Coursera, Udemy, EdX |
| PEC/OEC | 20 credits | SWAYAM, NPTEL |

## Approval Requirement

The student must obtain prior approval from the concerned Department Undergraduate Program Committee (DUPC).
