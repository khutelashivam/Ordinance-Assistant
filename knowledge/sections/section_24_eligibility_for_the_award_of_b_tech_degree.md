# Eligibility for the Award of B.Tech Degree

## 24.1
<!-- source-pages: 16 -->

A student shall be declared to be eligible for the award of B. Tech degree if he/she has :
a) Completed all the credit requirements for the
degree passing
all
the
credit
courses
/requirements mentioned in the course structure at the time of joining the programme.
b) satisfactorily completed all the non-credit requirements for the degree.
c) no dues to the Institute, Department, Hostels, NSS,NCC etc.; and
d) no disciplinary action is pending against him/her

## 24.2
<!-- source-pages: 16 -->

The award of B. Tech. degree must be recommended by the Senate and approved by the Board of
Governors/Governing Body of the Institute.
