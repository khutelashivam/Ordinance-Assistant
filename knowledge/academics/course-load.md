# Course Load and Fast and Slow Learning

## Flexible Course Timing

The semester mentioned for the following courses is indicative and suggested:

- Program Elective Courses (PECs)
- Open Elective Courses (OECs)
- Non-Mandatory AECs
- Non-Mandatory VACs

These courses may be completed earlier or later than the suggested semester.

## Prerequisites

If a student wishes to take a course earlier than its scheduled semester, the student must consider the prerequisite requirements.

The student must ensure that the prerequisite requirements of the course are satisfied.

## Course Availability

The student must also ensure that the desired course is offered in a free slot in the semester in which the student wants to take it.

## Maximum Semester Credit Load

The maximum number of credits a student may take in one semester, including backlog credits, is:

**32 credits**
