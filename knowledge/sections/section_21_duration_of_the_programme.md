# Duration of the Programme

## 21.1
<!-- source-pages: 16 -->

Normally a student should complete all the requirements for undergraduate programme in eight
semesters.
However, academically weaker students who do not fulfill some of the requirements in their first
attempt and have to repeat them in subsequent semesters may be permitted up to 12 consecutive
semesters (from the first semester registration) to complete all the requirements of the degree.

If a student, makes a re-entry (After Availing Certificate, Diploma, or B.Voc) in the Autumn
Semester after one or two breaks (at least two semester duration), the total duration shall be
maximum of 7 years.
