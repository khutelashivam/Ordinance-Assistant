# Residence and Hostel Requirements

## 10.1 Hostel Accommodation

Students who are assigned hostel accommodation by the Institute authorities must:

- Reside in the allotted room.
- Avail the Mess facility.
- Pay Hostel Rent.
- Pay other applicable hostel-related fees.

## 10.2 Relaxation of Fees

Under special circumstances, the Director may relax:

- Hostel rent
- Hostel-related fees
- Mess Fee

## 10.3 Married Accommodation

No married accommodation shall be provided to any student of the undergraduate courses.

## 10.4 Hostel Rules

All students must abide by the rules and regulations of the hostel as framed from time to time by the Hostel Affairs' Board.
