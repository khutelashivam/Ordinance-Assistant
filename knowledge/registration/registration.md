# B.Tech Course Registration

Every student must register for approved courses through the assigned Faculty Advisor at the beginning of each semester on the date specified in the Academic Calendar.

The Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs may cancel registration for one or more courses if the registration violates applicable rules.

Students are required to be physically present in their respective departments during registration.

A student is permitted to register only when:
- previous-semester fees and fines, if any, have been cleared;
- required fees for the current semester have been paid; and
- the student has not been debarred from registration on a specific ground.

A student must pass all first-year courses before registering for third-year (5th semester) courses.

Students admitted to the first year must submit all documents prescribed by the Institute at registration.

A student who misses the scheduled registration may be allowed late registration within the notified period for a compelling reason and on payment of the prescribed late fee. Normally, late registration is not permitted after one week or after the period specified in the Academic Calendar.
