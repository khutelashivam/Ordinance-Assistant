# B.Tech Admission

The number of seats in each undergraduate branch is decided by the Senate and approved by the Governing Body/Board of Governors of IIIT Bhagalpur.

Reservation is governed by applicable Ministry of Education/Government of India guidelines.

Admission to the B.Tech programme is made in the Autumn Semester at the first-year level through JEE-Main, conducted by the National Testing Agency under the supervision of the Joint Admission Board. The Institute follows applicable Ministry of Education guidelines for undergraduate admission.

If, after admission, it is found that a candidate did not fulfil the requirements stated in the admission offer, the Senate Chairperson may revoke the admission on the recommendation of the Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs and report the matter to the Senate.

The Institute may also cancel a student's admission and require discontinuation of studies because of unsatisfactory academic performance or disciplinary grounds. Academic-performance criteria are governed by the B.Tech rules and regulations, while disciplinary matters are governed by the applicable student code and conduct rules.
