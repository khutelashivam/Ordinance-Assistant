# Residence

## 10.1
<!-- source-pages: 4 -->

The students who are assigned Hostel accommodation by the Institute authorities, must reside in the
allotted room and have to avail the Mess facility and have to pay the Hostel Rent and other Hostel
related fees.

## 10.2
<!-- source-pages: 4 -->

Under special circumstances, the Director may relax Hostel rent, Hostel Related fees or Mess Fee.

## 10.3
<!-- source-pages: 4 -->

No married accommodation shall be provided to any student of the undergraduate courses.

## 10.4
<!-- source-pages: 4 -->

All students must abide by the rules and regulations of the hostel as may be framed from time to
time by the Hostel Affairs’ Board.
