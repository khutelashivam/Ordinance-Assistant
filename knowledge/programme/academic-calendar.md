# Academic Calendar

The Institute academic session is divided into two regular semesters:

- Autumn Semester
- Spring Semester

The Autumn Semester normally begins in July/August and the Spring Semester normally begins in January.

Each regular semester has fourteen weeks for instruction. The final two weeks are designated for the End-Semester Examination, and one week during the semester is designated for the Mid-Semester Examination.

The summer vacation may include Summer School and supplementary examinations.

The Institute's Academic Calendar specifies dates for activities such as:
- Orientation
- Registration and Late Registration
- Commencement of Classes
- Adding and Dropping Courses
- Document submission
- Examinations
- Submission of Grades
- Vacation
- Mid-Semester Recess
- Official student activities

The Academic Calendar is approved by the Senate and is notified for each semester or academic year by the Dean/Associate Dean (Academic).
