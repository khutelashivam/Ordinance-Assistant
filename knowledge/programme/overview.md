# B.Tech Programme Overview

The B.Tech programme at IIIT Bhagalpur is governed by the rules and regulations in the B.Tech Ordinances. The ordinance applies to existing disciplines and to new disciplines introduced under the B.Tech programme.

A student becomes eligible for the B.Tech degree after fulfilling the prescribed academic requirements and required extra-academic activities.

The ordinance describes the four-year B.Tech programme and currently lists these branches:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)
- Mechatronics and Automation Engineering (MAE)
- Mathematics and Computing (MNC)

The provisions may also apply to new disciplines introduced in the future.

Once approved by the Governing Body/Board of Governors, this ordinance supersedes corresponding earlier academic rules and regulations and is binding on students.

The Senate retains the authority to modify or relax regulations from time to time. Changes affecting students admitted in earlier years are to be handled so that those students are not placed in an unfair situation.
