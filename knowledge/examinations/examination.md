# Examination

## 15.1 Regular Examinations

The Institute follows a system of continuous assessment for evaluating students.

For theoretical courses, assessment includes:

- Quizzes
- Assignments
- Tutorials
- Internal assessment
- Attendance
- Mid-semester examination
- End-semester examination

## Laboratory Courses

For laboratory courses, continuous assessment includes:

- Attendance
- Laboratory report submission
- Performance evaluation after each laboratory session
- End-semester examination
- Viva

## Project Evaluation

Project evaluation includes:

- Submission of the project report
- Seminar

## 15.2 Supplementary Examination

### Eligibility

Students who have been awarded any of the following grades are eligible for a supplementary examination:

- F
- I
- X

### Timing

The supplementary examination is conducted during **May–July (summer term)** every year.

Students must register for supplementary courses by:

- Paying the prescribed fees.
- Registering within the stipulated time in the Academic Calendar.

### Late Registration

For late registration, students must obtain approval from the Dean/Associate Dean (Academics) within **7 days** from the date of registration in the summer term specified in the Academic Calendar.

The late fee remains the same as that applicable to the Spring/Autumn semester.

### Supplementary Examination Weightage

For a supplementary examination:

- Internal assessment carries **20% weightage** and is carried over from the concerned semester.
- Marks obtained in the supplementary examination carry **80% weightage**.

These components are considered for preparation of the new grade.

### Maximum Credits

A student may appear for supplementary examinations for a maximum of:

**15 credits**

in one summer term.
