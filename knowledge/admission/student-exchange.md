# Student Exchange and Credit Transfer

A B.Tech student who is permitted to attend another institute or university in India or abroad under an exchange programme or other approved arrangement may have credits earned at that institution transferred as earned credits.

If the course or research work completed at the other institution has not been evaluated there, IIIT Bhagalpur may evaluate it for equivalent credit. The resulting earned credit is then credited to the student's academic record.

The exact credit-transfer treatment is therefore subject to the Institute's applicable approval and evaluation process.
