# Leave of Absence

## 9.1 Leave of Absence

### Short Duration Leave

If the period of leave is less than two weeks, the student must submit a prior application for leave to the Head of the concerned department.

The application must:

- Mention valid reasons for the leave.
- Include supporting documents.

The leave is granted by the Head of the department.

### Leave Likely to Exceed Two Weeks

If the period of absence is likely to exceed two weeks, a prior application for leave must be submitted through the Head of the Department to the Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs.

Supporting documents must be provided.

### Absence Exceeding Four Weeks

If a student remains absent for more than four weeks, the Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs may ask the student to drop the semester.

### Effect on Attendance

Leave of absence under Clauses 9.1(a) and 9.1(b) will **not be condoned for attendance**.

## 9.2 Responsibility to Inform Authorities

Before availing leave, the student is responsible for informing:

- The Warden of the hostel in which the student resides.
- The concerned instructors.

## 9.3 Relaxation in Exceptional Circumstances

In exceptional circumstances, the Director may relax any of the above requirements on the recommendation of the Dean/Associate Dean of Academic Affairs.
