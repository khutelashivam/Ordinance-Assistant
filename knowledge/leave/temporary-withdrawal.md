# Temporary Withdrawal

## 9.4 Eligibility for Temporary Withdrawal

A student admitted to an undergraduate programme may be permitted to withdraw temporarily for one semester or more on grounds such as:

- Prolonged illness
- Grave calamity in the family

The temporary withdrawal is subject to the conditions specified below.

## Application

The student must apply to the Institute within at least **four weeks** of:

- The commencement of the semester; or
- The date the student last attended classes,

whichever is later.

The application must:

- State the reasons for withdrawal.
- Include supporting documents.
- Include endorsement of the student's guardian.

## Completion Requirement

The Institute must be satisfied that, even after counting the period of withdrawal, the student is likely to complete the B.Tech degree requirements within the specified time limits.

## Outstanding Dues

The student must have no outstanding dues or demands with:

- Institute
- Hostel
- Department
- Library
- NSS
- NCC

## 9.5 Maximum Withdrawal

Normally, a student will be permitted only **one such temporary withdrawal** during the undergraduate programme.

The total temporary withdrawal period must not exceed **one year**.
