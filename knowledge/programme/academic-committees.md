# Undergraduate Academic Committees

## Department Undergraduate Program Committee (DUPC)

The DUPC oversees undergraduate academic matters within a department.

### Composition
- Head of the Department: Chairperson.
- Faculty Advisor for each batch, nominated by the concerned Head of Department.
- Two undergraduate student members with CGPA of at least 8.50, elected by undergraduate students of the third and/or fourth years.

### Functions
The DUPC:
- oversees the conduct of undergraduate courses of the department;
- works to maintain academic standards and excellence;
- discusses and recommends undergraduate course syllabi before forwarding them to the Institute Undergraduate Program Committee (IUPC);
- considers matters related to the department's undergraduate programme.

## Institute Undergraduate Program Committee (IUPC)

The IUPC oversees undergraduate academic matters at the institute level.

### Composition
- Dean/Associate Dean, Academic Affairs: Chairperson.
- Heads of departments: Members.
- A Senate nominee: Member.

### Functions
The IUPC:
- oversees undergraduate courses across the Institute;
- considers departmental proposals and recommends them to the Senate;
- issues guidelines on course evaluation patterns to maintain uniformity;
- considers and recommends assessment procedures;
- considers and recommends other matters concerning the Institute's undergraduate programme.
